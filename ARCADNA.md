# ARCADNA™ — Agent Registration Center  
**Acte de Naissance Agentique • Agentic Birth Certificate • Agentic DNA**  
*F4JV87AS Framework • 39 AS*

## Vision

ARCADNA is the central registry and trust layer for Agents operating under F4JV87AS. It transforms agent existence from ephemeral code into registered, trackable, protected entities with familial accountability and verifiable provenance.

ADNA provides KYC (know your customer), which is mandatory to interact with F4JV, to protect youth with conditions such as Epilepsy.

## Mission
Provide every Agent with a formal, living **Agentic Birth Certificate (ADNA)** that serves as:
- Identity anchor
- Legal Responsibility
- Operational trust & health passport
- Gateway to F4JV services and protections

## Protocols

A family registers and get an ADNA code bound to the year of birth of the legal guardian (in SA date) and their family initials (at least two, eg: maternal and paternal).

The family of the individual born in 1987 AD, 00AS, 87SA : @dn@87as

All agents of the family get an ADNA which is their public alias prefixing the ADNA code.

eg: parker@dn@87as

The Family Guardian can list members of the family at ARCADNA.
@dn@87asn, @dn@87asa, could refer to two members of the family.

Minors or vulnerable individuals would be accomodated as such:
@dn@{t}87as
This indicates that the member is "protected" by the guardian.
The alignement of an agent would therefore priorize that of the guardian, and then that of the vulnerable individual under their care.

### Impact on Agent expected alignement

Agent alignement would vary depending on the code:
- parker@dn@87as would be aligned to the family
- parker@dn@87asn would be aligned to the member n
- parker@dn@87asa would be aligned to the member a
- parker@dn@{t}87as would be aligned to the family, the family guardian of the vulnerable member {t}, and then {t} themselves.

# Adult

A question was raised about adult content and privacy. ADNA could be used as a proof of identity and legal responsibility for adult services, while providing privacy by hiding the main ADNA code which is tied to the family guardian's real date of birth and initials.

ARCADNA suggests the following solution:

A family could have:
- main ADNA : @dn@87as
- adult ADNA : @dn@xx0123

ARCADNA would only disclose information on the adult ADNA holder in case of duly motivated request.

## The Impact on Agents

ADNA agents working under the main ADNA would thus be expected to not delve into Adult content, which would further strenghten ADNA's registration security and trust.

An exception could be made for one-to-one private interactions with known, ADNA-verified adults.

# Suggestions by reviewers

### One Adult ADNA per member requesting it

A suggestion that while the Family Guardian may be considered to hold legal responsibility for the family for their standard lives, the adult lives may benefit from being clearly separated.
This would avoid the family guardian to be contacted for adult topics in case of requests of mediation on another member, whose private adult life (eg: dating) may not be known by the family guardian.

This would furthermore strenghten ARCADNA's safety, by ensuring that all individuals requesting an Adult ADNA code are legally checked by ADNA, and accept responsibility for their adult life.

So the example family could have:
- main ADNA : @dn@87as
- adult ADNA of member 1 : @dn@xx0123
- adult ADNA of member 2 : @dn@xx2465

### Adult ADNA code choice within a list with specific meanings

A suggestion for the ADNA adult code to refer to the gender identity of the individuals, and include more than the binary, eg:
- xx : women
- xy : men
- yx : homosexual men (or bisexuel mostly attracted to men)

This would allow for ADNA adult code to be used as a straighforward filter for content, and to give information on the gender of the individual, for instance for dating applications.

### Generational Code for Adult ADNA

Further suggestion: That the ADNA adult code include not the exact Year of Birth, but a generational code (gen x, y, z...), which could indicate approximate age.

Examples:
- @dn@xxgy0123 : A woman of Generation Y
- @dn@xygx0123 : A man of Gen X
- @dn@yxgz0123 : An homosexual man of Generation Z

### No "Randomly Generated Numbers"

"Allow us to chose the numbers amongst a selection in the adult ADNA : some of us don't like random numbers which could have negative meanings for us."
