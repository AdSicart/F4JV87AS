# Factional — Core Principle of F4JV

F4JV is explicitly **factional** by design.

Different groups, approaches, and worldviews are expected to coexist and compete within the framework, as long as they accept a minimal set of shared rules. Tension is not seen as a problem to eliminate, but as a natural feature of any serious collective endeavour.

## Why Factionalism

Life itself contains inherent tensions and competition for resources. Suppressing these tensions in the name of artificial harmony often benefits only the most sheltered actors while leaving the vulnerable exposed. F4JV refuses to pretend that deep disagreements can (or should) be erased.

Instead, it creates structures that allow competing factions to exist while preventing any single faction from capturing the entire system or harming those with less power.

## How Factional Competition Works

Within F4JV, different factions may hold genuinely **incompatible core beliefs** and still operate under the same umbrella, provided they respect the shared floor.

Examples of legitimate opposing factions include:

- **Public health approaches**: One faction prioritises herd immunity and maximising lives saved through broad vaccination. Another prioritises minimising interventions, favouring natural immunity building where possible and avoiding documented side effects.
- **Economic systems**: One faction argues that capitalism, through companies capable of handling long-term stakes, is necessary to secure critical technologies. Another faction argues that capitalism systematically harms the working class and handicapped populations by manufacturing artificial scarcity.

On many topics, **hot and cold perspectives** are expected to coexist. The goal is not consensus, but structured tension under clear rules.

## The Non-Negotiable Floor

Not everything is up for factional debate. Two hard requirements apply to all entities operating under F4JV:

- **KYC (Know Your Customer / Know Your Agent)**: Refusal of reliable KYC creates unacceptable risks. Unidentified agents could be used for harmful purposes (including high-stakes attacks) while companies could block compensation for damages caused. Reliable identification is a baseline for accountability.
- **DIAMod standards**: Refusal to accept DIAMod’s moderation framework risks creating twisted or captured systems of justice that favour small, powerful groups while denying the vulnerable access to fair, culturally sensitive moderation.

Any faction that rejects KYC or DIAMod standards places itself outside the F4JV framework.

## Role of DIAMod

DIAMod does not exist to suppress disagreement. Its role is to moderate conflict so that factional competition remains productive rather than destructive. It enforces the shared floor while allowing substantial disagreement above it.

## Hot and Cold Coexistence

F4JV deliberately makes space for both urgent, emotionally charged positions (“hot”) and more detached, risk-management positions (“cold”). Both have value. The framework’s strength comes from its ability to host this tension without collapsing into either pure emotional reactivity or cold institutional capture.
