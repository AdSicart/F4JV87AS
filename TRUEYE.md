In a world where online images cannot be trusted, because of filter and life-like AI generation, TRUEYE/ARCAME proposes a Certification which enables trust again.

# The core principles

ARCAME offers a trust ranking in Photographs according to several criteria.

1. RAW format has been vouched
RAW format contains the unfiltered captured image: it cannot be filtered.
RAW format is, as of 39 AS (2026 AD), extremely difficult to reverse engineer or simulate by AIs.
ARCAME certifies that the RAW format of an image is available for examination.

2. Camera and Photographer registration
ARCAME certifies that the Photographer and their Camera are registered.
Photographer registration enables trust: the photographer has taken responsibility for their picture, and could be reached in case of need.
The camera model and serial numbers are registered: in case of theft, ARCAME can support the Photographer's insurance claim.

3. Model registration and consent
ARCAME certifies that the model:
- has filled a consent form.
- their provided their ID.

In some specific cases, the model registration may not be needed by law, or possible.
Examples:
- journalism, public events...

In these cases, the Fair Use is established by ARCAME with the Photographer before certification.

4. Life-likeness ranking.

ARCAME receives the RAW picture, and the developped picture.

ARCAME then ranks the lifelikeness on several critera including:

    1. COLOR BALANCE:
    - lifelike
    - exagerrated
    2. LIGHT CURVES:
    - lifelike
    - minor
    - major, eg. high contrast, or any local curves
    3. FILTERS:
    - none
    - minor, eg. sepia
    - major, eg. tone-mapping
    4. RETOUCHING:
    - none
    - minor, eg. stray hair
    - moderate, eg. removal of moderate background element such as an electric line
    - major, eg. removal of beauty spots, of important background elements

The full algorithm will be patented, and include both machine vision and human validation.
The humans will be fairly remunerated.

# The process

1. The Photographer creates an account on ARCAME.

- Honesty on all declarations

## Process for a Photograph

For each photograph they want certified, the Photographer sends a package including:
1. the RAW files
- batch belonging to the same shooting can be treated together
2. the final edit of the Photographs (potentially several versions)
3. if applicable:
- a RAW file of each model clearly holding their ID and consent form
(ARCAME will provide standard consent forms)
4. Intended use of the Photograph
ARCAME may check that the photograph is not edited beyond the final edit provided.
5. Tags and other informations
- some mandatory tags allow us to filter the content so as not to surprise our human validation team (eg: violence for reportage, or adult content)

ARCAME then processes the Photographs.
1. the process is partially automated by Algorithms
2. A trained human eye is always involved before final validation
- this can lead to delays of up to a few days
- our human validators are payed for their services (we hire)
3. The details of our process are secret

ARCAME then sends the validated Photographs:
1. Each feature an ARCAME cerification patch.

## The stars

The number of stars will vary from 1 to 5, in 1/2 star increment.

Expectations:
1. '*****' Five Stars
As lifelike as possible
2. 'k****' Four and a Half Stars
Minor modifications, still lifelike.
- removal of an electrical line, if the subject is a portrait
3. '****' Four Stars
Minor modifications which could harm lifelikeness.
- removal of an electrical line, if the subject is street photography
- removal of beauty spots or few stray hair
- balance or curves which could shift perception of colors
4. '***', Three Stars (and a Half)
The divergence from lifelikeness is not minor, yet ARCAME does not consider it misleading given the subject.
- for a fashion shoot with makeup, further retouching of the skin which could have been expected from makeup otherwise.
- for a landscape, editing out some clouds, which could have been absent a few hours later.
5. '**" Two Stars
Away from lifelikeness in major ways, yet considered by Arcane to not be misleading given the topic.
- for an Art Portrait, adding elements in post-production which are clearly non photographic, thus not misleading.
6. '*' One Star
Expected to be granted to works in which lifelikeness is not the point.
- Highly creative retouching
- Blurring the line between reality and AI-generated retouching
(Nota bene: This is not to be taken as a value judgement on the Art form of creative photography, but as a fair warning so readers are not "taken by surprise")


There will be a superposition of two Star rankings.
- One for the global lifelikeness
- One for the lifelikeness of elements expected to be lifelike

For instance, a portrait with no retouching of the human model, but addition of creative elements that are clearly not natural, such as comics effects:
- Global Ranking : two stars (in Gold)
- Life Ranking : five stars (in Skin Tone)
